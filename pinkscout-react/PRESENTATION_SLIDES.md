## SLIDE 5: How PinkScout Works — The Shared Data Pool ⚡

### The Technical Magic, Made Simple

1. **Opt-in data sharing** — Your scouts + community scouts = richer dataset
2. **Your data weighted 2x** — Your observations ALWAYS take priority
3. **Outlier detection** — Bad data automatically removed
4. **Unified metrics** — EPA + The Blue Alliance + your scouting = one score

> **A team that scouted 10 matches gets insights from 200+ through the shared pool**
